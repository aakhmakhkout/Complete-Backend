# Backend Learning Roadmap

**Primary resource:** Sheryians Complete Backend One Shot  
**Video:** https://youtu.be/0IciwnJ6PJI  
**Stack:** JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose  
**Daily lesson target:** 30–50 minutes per day (video or supplementary learning)  
**Total daily study, including practice:** Approximately 3–4 hours

Follow the video chronologically. Each day, study only the assigned segment or supplementary topic for 30–50 minutes. If the listed video segment is shorter than 30 minutes, use the remaining lesson time to review that day's concepts or consult the linked official documentation. If a segment takes longer than 50 minutes to understand, split it across days rather than rushing. Use supplementary resources only for prerequisites or important topics the video does not cover deeply. Do not follow another complete backend course simultaneously.

## Daily study allocation

**The 30–50 minute limit applies to the day's lesson/video learning.** Practice is additional.

| Activity | Time |
|---|---:|
| Assigned video segment or supplementary lesson | 30–50 min |
| Practical coding and implementation | 90–120 min |
| Independent exercises | 30–45 min |
| Revision and debugging | 30 min |
| **Total** | **Approximately 3–4 hours** |

If the day's assigned video timestamps cover less than 30 minutes, do not jump ahead just to fill time. Revisit the concepts, code along again, or use official documentation for that same day's topics. Keep the lesson target to 30–50 minutes.
